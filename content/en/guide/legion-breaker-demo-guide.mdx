## Core Mechanics: Mastering Blight Damage and Spirit Drain

The combat loop in *Legion Breaker* differs significantly from typical tower defense or wave survival titles. Instead of relying solely on physical barricades or traditional health pools, your battlemage is constantly subjected to ambient environmental decay alongside direct enemy threats. Understanding how health attrition works is crucial before spending your hard-earned gold.

### The Blight Attrition System

From the moment a battle commences, your battlemage suffers constant damage over time, officially termed **Blight Damage**. This mechanic acts as both a soft combat timer and an attrition gate:

- **Environmental Degeneration:** Even if zero enemies reach your position, your health bar steadily drains each second.
- **Scaling Threat:** Each successive battlefield increases the incoming Blight rate, forcing you to either accelerate your kill count or expand your sustain pool.
- **Direct Collision Damage:** Any undead minion that manages to cross the line deals catastrophic direct hits, which can instantly wipe out unupgraded mages.

### Offsetting Blight with Spirit Drain

To counterbalance Blight Damage, the upgrade tree offers **Spirit Drain**. This passive ability restores a set amount of Hit Points every time an enemy unit dies. Because Spirit Drain triggers on kill rather than on damage dealt, efficient area-of-effect spells become your primary life-support system. 

If you target high-health priority targets while ignoring swarms of weaker fodder, your health bar will collapse from Blight. High-level play requires clustering weak enemies together, detonating them to replenish HP, and maintaining that health surplus while burning down elite foes.

| Mechanic | Type | Function | Tactical Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Blight Damage** | Constant Drain | Depletes player HP automatically every second | Mitigation requirement for every stage |
| **Spirit Drain** | On-Kill Healing | Restores fixed HP when any enemy perishes | Essential engine for sustain in mid-to-late waves |
| **Fortitude** | Max HP Buffer | Expands baseline Hit Point pool | Prevents sudden deaths and affords time for projectile travel |
| **Direct Impact** | Collision Damage | Enemies reaching baseline deal lethal bursts | Requires frontline infantry to intercept runners |

### 1. Offensive Magic: Fireball and Resounding Fragments

Your primary active role in *Legion Breaker* is aiming and casting magical volleys. Investing in offensive perks transforms basic lobbed shots into cascading room-clearing explosions:

- **Fireball Upgrades:** Increase blast radius, base damage, and projectile travel speed. Faster projectiles mean faster kills, which translates to immediate healing via Spirit Drain.
- **Resounding Fragments:** Causes primary impacts to fracture into secondary shrapnel. These bouncing fragments trigger cascading hits across packed packs of undead, generating rapid multi-kills.

### 2. Defensive Foundation: Fortitude vs. Spirit Drain

A common dilemma discussed in any **Legion Breaker demo guide** is whether to favor raw Hit Points (Fortitude) or sustain-on-kill (Spirit Drain). Community reports indicate that relying exclusively on Spirit Drain while skipping Fortitude can result in painful run failures on later stages. 

Without sufficient maximum HP, the initial Blight tick on fresh maps can drain your life pool before your first spell even lands. Aim to keep Fortitude upgrades balanced alongside your drain tiers.

### 3. Assembling the Army: Oathsworn Guard and Temple Huntsmen

Automated support units relieve micro-management burdens, allowing you to concentrate your manual aiming on high-threat targets:

- **Oathsworn Guard (Spearmen):** Frontline infantry that stab advancing undead. They act as an insurance policy against fast stragglers that slip through your spell radius. Note that reaching advanced military tiers requires purchasing prior Oathsworn Guard tiers.
- **Temple Huntsmen (Archers):** Backline marksmen that automatically loose arrows at oncoming targets. While they lack the massive burst damage of a well-placed fireball, their constant chip damage softens targets, ensuring your spells achieve lethal one-shot thresholds.

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough: Defeating the 3 Demo Bosses

The *Legion Breaker* demo features three distinct boss battles, culminating in a showdown with an Evil Wizard. Each encounter tests a different aspect of your build, from crowd control to burst damage.

### Boss 1: The Undead Chieftain

The first boss tests your foundational understanding of the firing rhythm and target prioritization. The Chieftain enters accompanied by slow-moving skeleton infantry.

- **Strategy:** Do not focus all fire on the boss immediately. If you fire exclusively at the Chieftain, your health will drain from Blight before his sizable health pool drops.
- **Tactic:** Alternately target the smaller skeletons marching in front. Scoring kills keeps your Spirit Drain ticking, keeping your HP capped while secondary splash damage chips away at the Chieftain.
- **Minimum Recommended Stats:** Fortitude I, Spirit Drain I, Base Spell Velocity boost.

### Boss 2: The Swarm Vanguard

The second boss encounter escalates enemy density significantly. Fast-moving ghoul packs sprint down the lanes, attempting to overwhelm your firing cooldown.

- **Strategy:** Position your aiming reticle slightly ahead of the incoming pack to account for projectile arc and flight time. 
- **Tactic:** Purchase Resounding Fragments before this fight. When the primary projectile strikes the front-runner, the ricocheting fragments will wipe out trailing runners, generating massive waves of healing and gold income.
- **Minimum Recommended Stats:** Fortitude II, Spirit Drain II, Resounding Fragments I, Oathsworn Guard I.

### Boss 3: The Evil Wizard

The final challenge of the demo pits you against a powerful dark spellcaster. Unlike previous brute encounters, this boss summons waves while staying back, letting heavy attrition wear you down.

- **Strategy:** You cannot defeat this encounter without sufficient health reserves. Player experience highlights that entering this stage with only base HP often leads to defeat within seconds due to the accelerated Blight rate.
- **Tactic:** Deploy Temple Huntsmen to provide continuous single-target damage directly onto the Evil Wizard. Use your manual fireball volleys exclusively to obliterate minion summon packs, fueling Spirit Drain to survive the encounter's high passive Blight damage.
- **Minimum Recommended Stats:** Fortitude III (8 total HP), Spirit Drain III, Temple Huntsmen I, Max Projectile Radius.

| Boss Encounter | Main Threat | Primary Counter | Target HP Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Boss 1: Chieftain** | High HP pool & steady advance | Cleaving minions for Spirit Drain recovery | 5 HP (Fortitude I) |
| **Boss 2: Vanguard** | Fast swarm speed | Resounding Fragments shrapnel proc | 6–7 HP (Fortitude II) |
| **Boss 3: Evil Wizard** | Extreme Blight rate + summoned waves | Temple Huntsmen DPS + Minion farming | 8+ HP (Fortitude III) |

## Advanced Strategies and Avoiding Attrition Softlocks

Because the demo uses a semi-linear upgrade tree and autosaves your state, understanding gold economy and resource allocation is vital to avoiding difficult dead-ends.

### Beware the Minimal HP Softlock

In community discussions on itch.io, players experimenting with glass-cannon builds discovered a tricky situation: skipping Fortitude completely to chase pure damage upgrades can create an unwinnable position on the final battlefield.

If you arrive at the third boss with only 3 baseline HP:
1. The ambient Blight Damage ticks so rapidly that you have roughly 2 to 3 seconds of life.
2. If your initial projectile takes 2.5 seconds to travel across the battlefield, you may die before your first kill procs Spirit Drain.
3. Because completed stages cannot be endlessly farmed for free gold in the demo build, you could end up trapped without the funds to buy Fortitude.

To avoid this, **always reserve enough gold to upgrade Fortitude alongside your damage spells**. Prioritize safety over greed whenever entering a new tier of stages.

### Optimizing Spell Firing Rhythm

Unlike auto-shooters where weapons fire instantaneously, *Legion Breaker* incorporates deliberate travel time and firing cadences. 

- **Lead Your Shots:** Fireballs travel along an arc. Always place your targeting reticle two enemy paces ahead of moving hordes.
- **Track the Invisible Cadence:** While future versions plan visual reload indicators, current versions require you to feel out the weapon cadence. Avoid clicking erratically; time your cursor placement to match the moment your wizard completes the casting cycle.
- **Cluster Collisions:** Wait until two enemy lines compress before lobbing your spell. Hitting an overlap triples the value of Resounding Fragments.

| Playstyle Approach | Strengths | Vulnerabilities | Best Suited For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Glass Cannon (Pure Magic)** | Incredible screen-wipe damage | Extreme vulnerability to high Blight rates | Early waves (Battlefields 1–2) |
| **Juggernaut (HP / Sustain)** | Unkillable against steady decay | Slower boss kill times | High Blight encounters |
| **Combined Arms (Army + Magic)** | Balanced autonomous defense & burst | Highest overall gold investment cost | Boss 3 (Evil Wizard) conquest |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to beat the Legion Breaker demo?
Most players complete the demo in roughly 30 to 60 minutes. The run length depends on your upgrade path efficiency and whether you successfully avoid attrition softlocks on the final boss.

### Can you refund upgrade tree points if you make a mistake?
In current demo versions (v0.6.1), upgrade purchases are permanent upon spending gold. Because of this, following a structured **Legion Breaker demo guide** is recommended so you do not allocate all funds into offense while leaving your Hit Points critically under-leveled.

### What is the most critical upgrade in this Legion Breaker demo guide?
While Resounding Fragments provides the most satisfying visual clear, **Spirit Drain** paired with **Fortitude** is the absolute backbone of any winning run. Without Spirit Drain, surviving the passive Blight Damage across later encounters is mathematically impossible.

### Will demo save files carry over to the full game?
The demo features an automatic save system allowing you to pause and resume within your browser or Windows client. While demo saves allow you to finish the three bosses at your own pace, save compatibility with the full release will depend on future updates and balance changes made prior to launch.
