## Advanced Strategies to Crush the Necromancer King's Legions

Reaching the final battlefields requires mastering battlefield positioning and skill synergy. Even when piloting the **Legion Breaker best build**, reckless upgrade timing during live combat can result in a quick defeat if a boss summons elite reinforcements unexpectedly.

### Tip 1: Time Upgrades Between Waves
In an active incremental game, spending resources mid-combat can momentarily break your attention. Whenever a boss warning appears on screen, bank your gold for two waves prior. The instant the boss steps onto the field, dump your accumulated wealth into immediate combat buffs: temporary attack speed bursts or emergency frontline armor reinforcements.

### Tip 2: Funnel Undead Into Consecrated Zones
Battlefields expand as you conquer stages, spreading out enemy spawns. Rather than letting your holy troops roam freely, use the rally command to hold a central defensive triangle. This forces undead mobs to cluster closely together, directly amplifying the area damage of your *Arcane Rupture* and *Consecrated Earth* spells.

### Tip 3: Prioritize Lich Summoners
Boss encounters feature regular summoning waves that shield the primary objective. Always direct your wizard's targeted spells at backline necromancers before redirecting fire back to the central boss. Eliminating summoners removes healing auras from the undead front, enabling your holy army to push the line forward.
