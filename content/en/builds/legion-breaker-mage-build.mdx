## Step-by-Step Campaign Roadmap: Novice to Necromancer King

Navigating the full campaign demands continuous adaptation. Early battlefields forgive inefficient spending, but late-game encounters feature armored monstrosities that laugh at unoptimized spell kits. Follow this phased progression strategy to keep your **Legion Breaker mage build** ahead of the power curve.

### Phase 1: The Outskirts (Zones 1–3)
Start by unlocking Arcane Spark's split-shot upgrade. Avoid sinking gold into advanced troop classes during this phase; basic Footmen are inexpensive and sufficient to stall introductory skeleton patrols. Focus 70% of earned gold into wizard cast speed and base damage.

### Phase 2: The Blighted Swamps (Zones 4–6)
Undead hordes become noticeably denser, featuring poison-spitting ghouls. Unlock Solar Flare and recruit Shieldbearer Paladins to anchor the frontline against charging brutes. Begin allocating 30% of your gold into troop health upgrades to prevent instant squad wipes.

### Phase 3: The Citadel Gates (Zones 7–9)
Skeletal mages and siege engines appear, threatening your wizard from afar. Pivot toward Chain Lightning with bouncing upgrades to counter long-range artillery units. Integrate Battle Priests into your army composition to sustain your Paladins through intense attrition battles.

### Phase 4: The Throne of the Necromancer King (Zone 10)
The final encounter pits your forces against hundreds of simultaneous summons and multi-phase boss attacks. Maximize Critical Strike Multipliers on your primary spells and activate Consecration on cooldown. Keep your holy army reinforced continuously to intercept boss charge phases while your wizard channels sustained arcane barrages.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most effective Legion Breaker mage build for beginners?
The Elemental Annihilator is widely considered the most forgiving build for new players. By focusing early gold on Solar Flare and Chain Lightning, you achieve massive screen-wide clear capabilities without relying on complex micromanagement, giving your starter holy troops plenty of breathing room.

### Should I prioritize wizard damage or army upgrades first?
In the early game, wizard damage is far more cost-effective. Player experience shows that spending gold on wizard attack speed and base damage yields higher clearspeeds initially. Once you reach mid-tier battlefields (Zone 4 and beyond), you should split your income roughly 60/40 between spell enhancements and troop defensive stats.

### Can an arcane-focused Legion Breaker mage build defeat the Necromancer King?
Yes, an Arcane Barrage setup excels at boss execution due to rapid single-target DPS and armor-piercing keystones. However, you must pair it with high-tier defensive troops like Shieldbearer Paladins to keep skeletal summons away from your wizard during the boss's summoning phases.

### How do I manage screen lag during large-scale late-game battles?
As hundreds of troops and skeletons clash simultaneously, visual clutter can peak. You can toggle off damage numbers and reduce particle intensity in the options menu to maintain smooth frame rates during climactic, army-wide magical explosions.
