## Core Gameplay: From Novice Wizard to Holy Battlemage

The central loop of the game revolves around exponential character growth. You start a run wielding a single low-impact projectile spell. Skeletons crumble under your early assaults, dropping gold coins that you vacuum up to fuel immediate battlefield enhancements.

As enemy waves thicken, casting single magic bolts no longer suffices. You must tap into the game's branching progression trees to summon frontline crusaders, holy paladins, and defensive wardens. These troops actively form defensive perimeters, keeping skeletal swarms at bay while your wizard channels apocalyptic board-clearing spells.
