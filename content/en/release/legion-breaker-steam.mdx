## Wizard Progression and Army Synergy: Building the Ultimate Battlemage

Success in the late-game stages requires more than simply spamming a single spell. You must craft synergistic loadouts, creating builds that players on the **Legion Breaker steam** discussion forums frequently recommend for tackling elite bosses.

### 1. The Elemental Spell Disciplines
Your wizard begins with basic magic missiles, but the massive skill tree quickly branches into distinctive schools:
* **Arcane Mastery:** Focuses on pure piercing damage, projectile velocity, and cooldown resets. Ideal for snipping necromancers hiding behind frontlines.
* **Pyromancy:** Introduces lingering burning ground, explosive splash radii, and armor-melting passive debuffs.
* **Cryomancy:** Prioritizes crowd control, freezing charging hordes in place while your defensive line reorganizes.
* **Holy Radiance:** Deals bonus percentage damage to undead monstrosities and actively buffs adjacent friendly soldiers.

### 2. Mobilizing the Holy Crusade
Your wizard cannot survive prolonged physical contact with swarming skeletons. Recruiting holy units creates a living barricade that holds back the tide:
* **Crusader Footmen:** High physical resistance; built to plug narrow chokepoints.
* **Radiant Clerics:** Provide regenerative auras to sustain your frontline against decaying curses.
* **Silver Knights:** Elite shock cavalry that charge into dense throngs, staggering medium-tier mini-bosses.

| Unit Class | Primary Combat Role | Best Spell Synergy | Ideal Target Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shieldbearer** | Line retention and damage soaking | Cryomancy (Blizzard, Frost Armor) | Fast swarming minions |
| **Holy Paladin** | High-health cleave damage | Pyromancy (Meteor, Fireball) | Heavy armored skeletons |
| **Sun Cleric** | Area healing and anti-decay wards | Holy Radiance (Divine Light) | Cursed spirits and specters |
| **Templar Crossbowman** | Ranged physical armor piercing | Arcane Mastery (Chain Bolt) | Distant necromancer summoners |

Player experience suggests that investing heavily into early troop spawn rate allows you to bank gold safely during early waves, enabling a smooth transition into expensive late-tier magic spells without risking health loss.

## Recommended Progression Milestones

Navigating the massive passive tree can feel overwhelming during your initial runs. To maximize your gold efficiency, follow this phased upgrading framework:

```
Early Stage: Gold Find & Base Radius
       │
       ▼
Mid Stage: Unlock Holy Infantry & Cold Control
       │
       ▼
Late Stage: Arcane Piercing & Paladin Shields
       │
       ▼
Endgame: Screen-Wide Holy Storms & Boss Burst DPS
```

1. **Prioritize Economic Yield First:** Maximize base gold multiplier passives within the first three campaign stages. The earlier your economic engine runs, the faster you can unlock Tier 3 spells.
2. **Establish Line of Sight Control:** Always acquire at least one reliable freezing or staggering spell before tackling the third zone boss to prevent getting overrun by swarming banshees.
3. **Balance Unit Caps with Unit Quality:** Having twenty fragile militia is often less effective against mid-game bosses than four well-armored Paladins equipped with Holy resistance auras.
